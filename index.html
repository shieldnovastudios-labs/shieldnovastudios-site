Questions

Which tools do you use?

AI video tools such as Higgsfield (Kling, Veo), plus Canva and our own editing. A person writes the script and makes the final cut.

Is ad spend included?

No. You run the ad from your own Meta or TikTok account. We never need your payment details.

Will people know it’s AI?

Yes, when it looks realistic. We add the AI label Meta and TikTok ask for.

Can the presenter look like a real person?

No. We don’t copy real faces or voices. Presenters are generated, or we use your own footage.

What won’t you make?

Fake reviews, fake “customer” videos, made-up results, and health or supplement claims.

Tell us what you sell.

Send a link and one line about the ad. We reply with a fixed quote and a date.
